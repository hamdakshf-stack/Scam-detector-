 
Scam Detector🛡️ in C | Programming Fundamentals Project
Excited to share our team's Scam Detector, developed in C as part of our Programming Fundamentals (PF) project at NED University!
We built a terminal-based system to protect people from online scams. You just paste a suspicious message, and it gives you a Risk Score out of 100.
It checks for four types of scams:
🔹 Message Scam
Detect fake lottery, prize, OTP, bank account, and urgent messages.
Check for words like congratulations, winner, claim, and account blocked.
🔹 Location Scam
Detect messages asking to share live location, current location, or GPS location.
Protects you from strangers asking "where are you" and "send me location".
🔹 Surveillance Scam
Detect blackmail and spying messages like camera hacked, we are watching you, and leaked video.
Warns you before you get scared by fake threats.
🔹 Link Scam
Detect suspicious and shortened links like http://bit.ly, tinyurl, .xyz, .tk, and .top.
Stops you from clicking on dangerous links.
🔹 Report New Scam Word
Users can report a new scam word or number that is not already in the database.
It gets saved in a file and our database keeps getting smarter with every report. This makes the detector more powerful over time!
💾 Data Storage:All user-reported scam words are stored and managed through a text file.
The project also gave us practical experience with important C programming concepts, including strings, arrays, functions, if-else, loops, file handling, and scoring system
